Privacy Policy for MeetAlarm

Last updated: September 9, 2026

MeetAlarm ("the app", "we") is built to be as private as possible: everything the app does happens on your device, and nothing you do inside MeetAlarm is sent to us or to any third party. This page explains exactly what the app accesses and why.

What the app accesses

  • Calendar (EventKit). With your permission, MeetAlarm reads the events in the calendars you choose, so it can find your upcoming meetings and schedule alarms before them. MeetAlarm never creates, edits, or shares your calendar events with anyone — it only reads them, and only to decide when to ring an alarm. Deleting an event inside MeetAlarm deletes it from your calendar, the same as deleting it in the Calendar app would.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit). With your permission, MeetAlarm schedules real system alarms tied to your meetings. This stays entirely on your device and is never reported anywhere.
  • Notifications. MeetAlarm may show a local, on-device reminder about your free trial ending. This notification is generated and delivered entirely by your device — it is not a push notification and does not involve any server.
MeetAlarm has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs of any kind. Nothing you do in the app — your calendar data, your settings, your alarm preferences — ever leaves your device, and we do not share it with any third party, because we never receive it in the first place.

What the app stores

Your preferences (lead time before meetings, colors, which calendars are tracked, per-meeting alarm overrides) are stored only in your device's local app storage. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared — we don't have servers to share it with.

Data retention, revoking access, and deletion

  • How long data is kept. Everything MeetAlarm stores lives only in the app's local storage on your device, for as long as you keep the app installed. We hold nothing on any server, so there is nothing on our end to separately retain or expire.
  • Revoking Calendar or Notification access. You can turn off MeetAlarm's access at any time in your device's Settings app: Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars (or Notifications) → MeetAlarm, and switch access off.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling MeetAlarm removes all of its local data (preferences, per-meeting alarm settings, everything) from your device immediately. Since nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is no separate server-side copy to request deletion of — uninstalling is the complete deletion.

Accounts

MeetAlarm does not have user accounts. You never create a profile, and we never collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.

Subscriptions and payment

If you choose to subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple ID. We never see your payment details — no card numbers, no billing address, nothing. Apple's own Privacy Policy covers how they process that transaction.

Children's privacy

MeetAlarm is a productivity tool for scheduling meeting alarms and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and given the app collects nothing from anyone, there is nothing to collect in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
