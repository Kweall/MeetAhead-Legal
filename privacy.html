Privacy Policy for MeetAlarm

Last updated: October 23, 2026

MeetAlarm ("the app", "we") is developed and published by an independent individual developer (contact details are at the bottom of this page). It is built to be as private as possible: everything the app does happens on your device, and nothing you do inside MeetAlarm is sent to us. The only outside service it talks to is Apple's App Store, to check your subscription (see "Subscriptions and payment" below). This page explains exactly what the app accesses and why.

What the app accesses

  • Calendar (EventKit). With your permission, MeetAlarm reads the events in the calendars you choose: each event's title, start and end time, location, notes, link (URL), whether it lasts all day, and your own reply to the invitation (so that a meeting you declined gets no alarm). It reads them only on your device, to decide when to ring an alarm and to look for a call link (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and similar) in an event's link, location and notes, which it then shows in that meeting's details. This reading also happens in the background and when you run the optional Shortcuts action. MeetAlarm never creates or edits calendar events and never shares them with anyone. The one change it can make is a deletion you ask for: if you delete a meeting inside MeetAlarm, that single occurrence is removed from your calendar, the same as deleting it in the Calendar app would.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit). With your permission, MeetAlarm schedules real system alarms tied to your meetings. This stays entirely on your device and is never reported anywhere. The alarm's title is handed to iOS so that the system can show it; depending on your device it can appear on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on a paired Apple Watch. If you turn on "Hide Titles on the Lock Screen" in MeetAlarm's Settings, those places show only the word "Meeting".
  • Clipboard. Only when you touch and hold a call link in a meeting's details and choose "Copy Link", MeetAlarm places that link on the system pasteboard, where other apps may be able to read it. MeetAlarm never reads the pasteboard.
  • Notifications. MeetAlarm may show a local, on-device reminder about your free trial ending. This notification is generated and delivered entirely by your device — it is not a push notification and does not involve any server.
MeetAlarm has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs of any kind. Nothing you do in the app — your calendar data, your settings, your alarm preferences — ever leaves your device, and we do not share it with any third party, because we never receive it in the first place.

Data retention, revoking access, and deletion

  • What's stored, and for how long. Your preferences — lead time before meetings, colors, which calendars are tracked, per-meeting alarm overrides — and the information needed to keep your alarms up to date (such as the titles and start times of upcoming meetings, and the date you first opened the app, used to count the free trial) live only in the app's local storage on your device, for as long as you keep the app installed. Like other app data, this local storage is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) if you use them. The one exception is the date you first opened the app: it is also kept in your device's Keychain (and syncs through iCloud Keychain if you use it), so that deleting and reinstalling the app does not restart the free trial. MeetAlarm cannot erase it from inside the app; it stays until you erase the device or reset iCloud Keychain. It identifies nothing about you and is never sent to us. We hold nothing on any server, so there is nothing on our end to separately retain or expire.
  • Revoking Calendar, Alarm, or Notification access. You can turn off MeetAlarm's access at any time in your device's Settings app: Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → MeetAlarm for the calendar, Settings → Notifications → MeetAlarm for notifications, and Settings → Apps → MeetAlarm for alarms. Turning access off stops the app from reading your calendar, scheduling alarms or showing notifications; nothing else changes.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling MeetAlarm removes its local data (preferences, per-meeting alarm settings, stored meeting titles) from your device immediately; only the first-opened date described above stays in your Keychain. Since nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is no separate server-side copy to request deletion of.

Accounts and support email

MeetAlarm does not have user accounts. You never create a profile, and the app itself collects no name, email address or other personal identifier. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and your message; we use them only to reply and handle any follow-up, keep the conversation only as long as that takes, delete it when you ask us to, and do not share it with anyone. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

Subscriptions and payment

If you choose to subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple ID. We never see your payment details — no card numbers, no billing address, nothing. Apple's own Privacy Policy covers how they process that transaction.

To find out whether you are subscribed, the app asks the App Store on your device (through StoreKit). This exchange takes place between your device and Apple; nothing from it is sent to us. The free trial itself is counted on your device from the day you first opened the app (see above).

Your rights and choices

Because your calendar data is processed only on your device and never reaches us, we hold no personal data about you: there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete, and we will confirm this if you ask. Where the law requires a legal basis, the on-device processing happens at your request, through the iOS permissions you grant and can withdraw at any time (see above); replies to your emails rest on our legitimate interest in answering you. You may complain to your local data-protection authority.

No selling, no tracking

We do not sell or share personal information, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not use any data for advertising. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics through App Store Connect; they do not identify you.

Children

MeetAlarm is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, children included.
