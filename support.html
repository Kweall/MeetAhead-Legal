MeetAlarm Support

MeetAlarm turns the meetings in your calendar into real system alarms, so you notice them even with the phone on silent or in a Focus. Need help? Write to kweallapps@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

  • My meeting has no alarm. Open MeetAlarm once: it looks at your calendar for the next 7 days when the app is opened, in the background, and from the optional Shortcuts automation described in Settings. Check that the meeting's calendar is switched on in Settings, that the meeting's alarm is not muted (swipe the row), and that vacation mode is off. All-day events only ring if "Holidays" is switched on.
  • Where do I allow alarms? Settings → Apps → MeetAlarm, and allow Calendar access under Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. The "Test alarm" button in MeetAlarm's Settings checks that alarms work.
  • Google, Outlook, or other calendars. MeetAlarm shows every calendar on your iPhone. Add the account in Settings → Calendar → Accounts and make sure Calendars is switched on for it.
  • A Google (or other) calendar meeting is missing or late. MeetAlarm reads the calendars on your iPhone. iOS fetches Google and some other accounts on its own schedule, so a new or moved meeting can take a few minutes to arrive; opening the Calendar app asks iOS to sync right away, and pulling down on the meeting list in MeetAlarm reloads it. An alarm can only be set for a meeting once iOS has it.
  • Subscription. The first 30 days are free. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the App Store app.
  • My data. Everything stays on your device; see the Privacy Policy.
